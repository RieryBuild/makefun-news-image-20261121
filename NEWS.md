## How the image was made

panel, no baked-in text, no photorealism); the world map, the numbered pins
and the Bahrain/Hormuz diagram strip are schematic shapes drawn in code.
The page was composed at 1024×1024 with a script (not submitted) and the
result is entry.png (486,111 bytes). main.ts (Node 22, standard library only)
validates entry.png (PNG magic, IHDR 1024×1024, IEND, ≤1 MiB) and copies it
to $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png; two runs are byte-identical.
