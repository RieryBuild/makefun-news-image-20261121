# NEWS.md — Saturday 21 November 2026: first-report dates and figure choices

Every story on the page happened, or was first reported, on Saturday
21 November 2026 in at least one timezone. Figures were verified from
primary sources at build time (22 Nov ~09:00–10:00 WITA); no figure is
taken from another entry, and no date comes from an aggregator's
republication stamp. bloggersminds.com was never used.

## The 13 pictured stories

1. **COP31 runs into overtime — Antalya.** Sat 21 Nov (AP, Reuters).
   Day 13; the summit missed its Friday close. The Thursday draft, cut
   to 80 paragraphs with no mention of fossil fuels and no $120bn
   adaptation-finance target, split nearly 200 countries; a third draft
   was promised for Saturday morning. EU Commissioner Hoekstra: "either
   going to be a good deal or no deal."
2. **Five missiles over Saudi Arabia.** Coalition statement Sat 21 Nov
   ~02:00 local: air defences destroyed 3 ballistic missiles over
   Riyadh before dawn (AFP heard two explosions; no casualties). The
   other 2 were destroyed over Jizan in the Friday-evening salvo,
   reported in the same coalition cycle; the page totals the coalition's
   two salvos as 5 (3 Riyadh + 2 Jizan), stating the split in the body.
   A Houthi drone headed south was also destroyed on Saturday.
3. **Peru quake M6.7.** 00:09 UTC Sat 21 Nov (19:09 Fri local), USGS
   magnitude 6.7, depth 108 km, 9 km NE of Carhuamayo (34 km north of
   Junin per AP). Peru's Geophysical Institute put the depth at 129 km;
   the page uses the USGS figure. Felt in Lima; no deaths, damage or
   tsunami risk (USGS, IGP).
4. **Zelensky: Crimea bridge "will not stay".** NBC interview given
   20 Nov, reported Sat 21 Nov (Kyiv Post 06:54 UTC). He warns of
   double-tap strikes and says Russia attacked Ukrainian bridges
   109 times; the Kerch span was hit on 17 Nov.
5. **Iran says it shot down a US drone.** Sat 21 Nov, IRGC claim via
   IRIB (published 10:36 UTC): an MQ-9 downed over the Strait of
   Hormuz, the 58th such claim. No confirmation from Washington — shown
   as CLAIM, not fact.
6. **Iran: uranium stock stays in the country.** Foreign Minister
   Araghchi's remarks in the state newspaper *Iran*, published Sat
   21 Nov: a "fair solution" is possible, but the enriched-uranium
   stockpile will not leave Iran. Talks continue via mediators.
7. **Trump vows tax on data centres abroad.** White House briefing
   Fri 20 Nov, reported widely Sat 21 Nov: a "tremendous tax" on US
   firms building data centres overseas. No rate, no legal mechanism,
   no executive order published by 21 Nov.
8. **China tells Japan to "correct" Taiwan words.** Foreign Ministry
   spokesman Guo Jiakun's briefing Fri 20 Nov, reported Sat 21 Nov,
   after Xi Jinping snubbed PM Takaichi at the APEC summit in Shenzhen.
   Tokyo says its stance under the 1972 communique is unchanged.
9. **Jet lands safely after engine fire alert — Davao.** Sat 21 Nov
   ~00:08 local. Air Niugini flight PX19 (Hong Kong–Port Moresby),
   a 767 with 137 passengers and 9 crew, diverted on a right-engine
   fire indication; all 146 aboard safe, evacuated by slide. No
   injuries (CAAP, airline).
10. **Australia and China agree to AI talks.** Albanese–Li Qiang
    meeting Fri 20 Nov in Beijing; the joint statement and the new AI
    dialogue (safety, governance, application) reported Sat 21 Nov
    (ABC, pm.gov.au).
11. **Bitcoin below $59,000.** CoinGecko, early Saturday: $58,983 at
    02:08 UTC, down 3.9% in 24 hours; ether $1,625, down 6.1%.
    Cross-checked against CoinGecko's own hourly series at build time:
    $59,093 at 02:00 UTC, $58,416 at 03:00 UTC — the 02:08 figure
    sits inside that bracket. One timestamped figure is used.
12. **Verstappen inherits Las Vegas pole.** Qualifying ran Friday
    night in Las Vegas (Sat 21 Nov UTC). Antonelli fastest, 1:33.893,
    but a five-place grid penalty for power-unit changes drops him to
    sixth; Verstappen inherits pole and Russell starts second
    (FIA, Formula 1). The race (Sat 20:00 local) had NOT been run at
    build time — no race result is claimed anywhere on the page.
13. **Rahul 124\* at tea.** Sat 21 Nov, day three at Wellington:
    KL Rahul unbeaten on 124 off 265 balls, India 344 for 2 replying
    to New Zealand's 368. He was later out for 136 on day four — that
    later score is deliberately NOT used.
