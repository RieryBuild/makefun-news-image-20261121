  5 mixes in 2 more over Jizan on 20 Nov.

  police figure.

## Method

Every number on the page was checked against a primary outlet (Reuters, AP,
AFP, BBC, Kyiv Independent, ESPNcricinfo, F1/Pirelli, ESA, ATP, USGS, Crisis
Group, CoinGecko) on 21 November 2026. No figure was taken from another
entry, a leaderboard summary, or an aggregator's republication date. The
13 panel illustrations are original flat editorial drawings made for this
entry; the map is a hand-coded schematic. The page was composed with PIL
(`compose.py`, not submitted) and quantized to a 32-colour palette to hold
the file under 300 KB.
